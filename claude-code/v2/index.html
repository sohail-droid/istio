Chapter one

How we got here

  1. ✨

    The day we met

    I remember what you were wearing, what you said first, and the exact moment I stopped paying attention to anything else in the room.

  2. ☕

    The first ordinary day

    Groceries, bad weather, nothing planned. It was the day I realised I didn't need an occasion to want you around.

  3. 🌍

    Every adventure since

    Wrong turns, missed trains, terrible photos we refuse to delete. You turn logistics into stories.

  4. 🏡

    Becoming home

    Somewhere along the way you stopped being a person I love and became the place I come back to.

  5. 💍

    Today

    Which brings us to this page, and to the only question I've ever been nervous to ask.

Chapter four

A letter, before the question

Dear you,

I've rewritten this more times than I'll admit, because nothing I write is quite as convincing as the last few years have been. So here's the plain version: I like who I am next to you. I like the life we've quietly built while we were busy doing ordinary things.

I'm not asking because I think marriage will change anything. I'm asking because I don't want it to. I want more of exactly this — the trips and the Tuesdays, the plans and the pauses — for as long as I get.

Always yours,
Me
